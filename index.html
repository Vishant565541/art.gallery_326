Curator's Note

Small rooms
make room.

“Most talent doesn't need discovering. It needs a wall, a caption, and someone willing to hit repost.”

Gallery 326 started as a single feed post and a promise: every artist who sends work gets looked at by a person, not a filter. We hang a new hand-picked selection each week, credit every artist in full, and link straight back to their own page.

No submission fee. No exclusivity clause. No algorithmic gatekeeping. Just a rotating wall, an audience that showed up for the art, and a screening room for the process behind it.
